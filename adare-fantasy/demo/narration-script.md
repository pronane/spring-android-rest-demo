# Narration script

Ten beats, eleven audio files, spoken over `twenty-eight-start-to-finish.webm` (3:07). Each clip is
delayed to its start time and mixed into one Opus stream at render time, so the picture and the
captions are never re-encoded — see `README.md` → Narration.

The last clip was cut in two at a pause in the recording: `10a` covers the console and `10b` holds the
closing line over the card at the end.
